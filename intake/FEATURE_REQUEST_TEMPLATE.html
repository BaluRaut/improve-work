Guidance for requestors

  • Describe the problem and desired outcome, not a preferred technology.
  • Separate essential needs from nice-to-haves.
  • Include evidence wherever possible; do not invent metrics.
  • A requested date is not a delivery commitment. Explain why it matters.
  • Unknown answers are acceptable at intake and should become discovery questions.
